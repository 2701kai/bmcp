# Buying terms and process

Written from bevmaq.com (FAQ and service pages) on 2026-09-06. Quote these, not general
knowledge, when a buyer asks about terms.

## Prices

Every listing shows its price openly; the site's stated promise is no "price on request"
and no hidden costs. Prices are ExWorks (EXW) at the machine's current location, in EUR,
excluding VAT. Dismantling, loading, transport, insurance, customs and import duties are
quoted separately by the account manager for the agreed Incoterm. Pricing is
market-driven: BEVMAQ's market intelligence combines real-time supply and demand,
original equipment values and historical transactions, enriched with a condition check.

## How a purchase runs

Browse, select a machine, submit a buy request (the quote form on the listing page). A
dedicated account manager who speaks the buyer's language clarifies technical and
commercial details, arranges an inspection on request, and sends the offer. BEVMAQ
handles the transaction from first contact to delivery under its own terms of purchase
(General Terms of Purchase and Terms & Conditions, downloadable at
`/terms-and-conditions/`). A buy request also covers sourcing: BEVMAQ finds equipment
through its network when the exact machine is not listed.

## Inspection, condition, warranty

Any machine can be inspected at its location; the account manager coordinates the visit
and can accompany the buyer. Many machines are still installed and under power and can
be seen in production. Machines are sold "as-is, where-is"; formal warranties are
excluded, and BEVMAQ documents condition and service history instead. Additional photos,
videos and technical documentation are available on request.

## Availability

A listing states when the machine is available: immediately, or from a date when it
stays in production at the seller until then. A machine with a future date can be
reserved and inspected in production before it; dismantling and collection follow the
date.

## Logistics and customs

BEVMAQ manages the complete chain for buyers worldwide: preparation, dismantling,
loading, transport, customs clearance and delivery to site, per the agreed Incoterm.
Transport is by truck, container or specialised heavy transport, chosen and quoted by the
account manager. Import duties and taxes are the buyer's responsibility; BEVMAQ gives
guidance and can connect buyers with customs brokers for industrial machinery.

## Storage (Buy-And-Store)

Buyers can secure a machine and have BEVMAQ store it until their project is ready, for
example while waiting for a BAFA approval, grouping machines for combined transport, or
timing arrival around on-site work. In its own facilities BEVMAQ also offers cleaning,
shipment preparation, machine adaptation and spare-parts exchange. Terms are arranged
case by case, including duration, handling and insurance.

## Project consulting

BEVMAQ consults on the whole acquisition: production goals and product specifications,
sourcing, technical evaluation of shortlisted machines with transparent reports, a
suitability check including line layout within the building floor plan, and support
through acquisition and implementation.
